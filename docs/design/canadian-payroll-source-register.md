# Canadian payroll source-version register — 2026

**Retrieved:** 2026-10-24 UTC. **Status: SOURCE DISCOVERY ONLY.** This register records official source identities and visible version/currency signals. It is not a payroll, tax, or legal review; it does not establish current law, applicability, a complete levy list, or qualified sign-off. No rates, amounts, formulas, legal interpretations, or province-specific “not applicable” conclusions are recorded here.

This is a companion to the [approved scope and source-to-requirement matrix](canadian-payroll.md) and the [A3 calculator contract draft](canadian-calculator-contract.md). The accepted launch boundary remains provincially regulated employment in AB, BC, MB, NB, NL, NS, ON, PE, and SK; Québec, territories, and federally regulated employment remain blocked. This register does not change that scope or make Canadian payroll calculation-ready.

## Reading rules

- **Source found** means that an official source was identified and a visible title/date signal was captured. It does not mean the source chain was checked through a release date.
- **Currency unresolved** means the retrieved copy has an older, conflicting, incomplete, or otherwise un-reconciled date signal. Do not activate rules from it.
- The required rule topics in the scope matrix remain overtime, vacation pay, public/statutory holidays, and termination/final pay. A qualified reviewer must map each rule to operative sections, effective dates, exceptions, and source versions. Topic pages in the scope matrix are discovery leads, not approvals.
- Missing a levy source, or finding a page that does not mention a levy, is not evidence of “no levy.” No levy is marked not applicable here.
- A WCB/compensation publication naming a year or industry schedule does not provide a reviewed employer-specific classification, assessable-wage base, account rate, or adjustment. Those inputs remain required before calculation.

## A3 coverage consequences

1. Keep the four provincial employment-standard components—overtime, vacation pay, public/statutory holidays, and termination/final pay—**unreviewed** until a qualified reviewer has mapped each supported scenario to the operative legal text and source version. The existing [scope matrix](canadian-payroll.md) is the topic-source crosswalk; this register does not certify its rule interpretations or page currency.
2. Keep all provincial levy applicability decisions **unreviewed**. Identified levy source candidates are not proof of employer applicability; unresolved provinces are not implicitly “zero” or “not applicable.”
3. Keep WCB/workers’ compensation calculation **blocked** until the current board source, employer classification, assessable-wage basis, account-specific rate, effective dates, and any applicable board adjustments are independently reviewed.
4. Use CRA edition/table identifiers as version metadata only. A source match is not an expected payroll result. Numeric examples and formula activation remain gated on authoritative, source-versioned inputs and qualified independent payroll/tax review.
5. This register changes no production code and does not satisfy A3 issue #15 acceptance. The contract remains a non-calculating schema draft; Canadian calculations and Canadian outputs remain blocked.
