## Component inventory

### 1. Company jurisdiction, US state codes, and fallback

- **Observed assumption / locations:** Canadian company jurisdiction uses country code `CA` and a province code; the same `CA` is California in the separate US state-tax registry. See [`payroll_jurisdiction_service.py` constants and supported codes, L16–20](../../app/services/payroll_jurisdiction_service.py#L16-L20), [`state_tax/__init__.py` registry and `get_engine`, L36–61](../../app/services/state_tax/__init__.py#L36-L61), and the jurisdiction request contract in [`routes/payroll_jurisdiction.py`, L27–42](../../app/routes/payroll_jurisdiction.py#L27-L42). US state selection has a generic fallback: absent or unknown codes resolve to a zero-rate generic state engine; see [`state_tax/__init__.py`, L46–57](../../app/services/state_tax/__init__.py#L46-L57) and [`state_tax/generic.py`, L18–46](../../app/services/state_tax/generic.py#L18-L46).
- **Current guard:** Pay-run creation resolves company jurisdiction by pay date and blocks an active Canadian jurisdiction with HTTP 422. With no jurisdiction history, the legacy US path remains active; a future-only jurisdiction also leaves pre-effective dates on that path. See [`create_pay_run`, L197–237](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L197-L237). Existing tests assert both blocked active-Canada and permitted no-history/future-only US behavior: [`test_canadian_payroll_run_jurisdiction.py`, L58–133](../../tests/test_canadian_payroll_run_jurisdiction.py#L58-L133).
- **Safe interim disposition:** Keep Canadian payroll calculation blocked. Do not interpret missing jurisdiction as confirmation that a company is US, and do not treat `CA` state-engine selection as a Canadian province selection.
- **A1 / reviewer blocker:** Confirm company province and employment scope, then define explicit jurisdiction dispatch and unsupported-code behavior. The present legacy fallbacks are not Canadian replacements.

### 2. Employee, pay-run, and pay-stub models and schemas

- **Observed assumption / locations:** `Employee` stores SSN last four, W-4 filing status and elections, US mailing/work/residence state fields, state allowances, local rate and state-rate overrides; see [`models/payroll.py`, `Employee`, L93–136](../../app/models/payroll.py#L93-L136). `EmployeeCreate` defaults to hourly, biweekly, and single filing status and exposes the W-4/state fields without Canadian tax fields; see [`schemas/payroll.py`, L13–48](../../app/schemas/payroll.py#L13-L48). Pay frequencies are weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, and monthly, with a 26-period fallback for unknown values ([`models/payroll.py`, L48–72](../../app/models/payroll.py#L48-L72)). `PayStub` stores federal/state tax, Social Security, Medicare, FUTA, SUTA, and state-other amounts ([`models/payroll.py`, L260–312](../../app/models/payroll.py#L260-L312)). `PayRun` stores a jurisdiction snapshot, draft/processed/void status, totals, and transaction link, but no payment status/date field ([`models/payroll.py`, L75–84 and L228–257](../../app/models/payroll.py#L75-L84)). The general-ledger home-currency default is USD ([`models/settings.py`, L75–76](../../app/models/settings.py#L75-L76)).
- **Current guard:** Employee create/update is blocked for an active Canadian jurisdiction, and the pay-run create guard is described above. The schemas themselves remain US-shaped; a request schema does not establish Canadian employee or tax data.
- **Safe interim disposition:** Do not repurpose W-4, SSN, work-state, residence-state, or state-withholding fields as Canadian fields. Preserve them for legacy US records only; keep Canadian employee setup unavailable until a reviewed Canadian model/schema is selected.
- **A1 / reviewer blocker:** Confirm employee types and frequencies. Define which identity, province-of-employment, earnings, deduction, employer-contribution, currency, and historical-snapshot data are required before choosing model replacements. Do not assume the broader `canadian-payroll.md` frequency list is the launch decision.

### 3. Federal and state calculation services

- **Observed assumption / locations:** [`payroll_service.py`](../../app/services/payroll_service.py) documents an IRS Pub 15-T / W-4 federal method and labels its bracket figures “2026-approximate” at L2–10. `federal_income_tax` annualizes by pay frequency and applies W-4-style inputs; an unrecognized filing status silently becomes `single` at L132–174. Supplemental federal withholding uses the service's configured flat/high tiers and threshold at L109–113 and L177–193. The same service contains hard-coded Social Security, Medicare, Additional Medicare, and FUTA parameters at L19–28 and the corresponding cap calculations at L245–287. State withholding, reciprocity, and state-specific other employee/employer charges are selected by `calculate_withholdings` at L290–454; absent an explicit SUTA rate it uses `SUTA_RATE`, configured with a `0.012` default in [`config.py`, L82–86](../../app/config.py#L82-L86). State tables explicitly identify their figures as approximate and flag limitations in [`state_tax/tables.py`, L1–28](../../app/services/state_tax/tables.py#L1-L28); reciprocity is described as representative rather than exhaustive in [`state_tax/reciprocity.py`, L1–29](../../app/services/state_tax/reciprocity.py#L1-L29).
- **Current guard / fail-closed status:** These are US calculations. The state engine's unknown/missing-code fallback is not fail-closed for Canadian tax. The separate [`canadian_payroll_rules.py`](../../app/services/canadian_payroll_rules.py#L1-L6) module says it has no formulas or seeded tax values and requires reviewed rule sets; its `PayrollRuleRegistry.resolve` rejects unsupported jurisdictions/dates or missing rule sets at L238–256. In this branch's production call sites, pay-run creation and gross-up call `calculate_withholdings`; the Canadian registry is not connected to those routes.
- **Safe interim disposition:** Do not run or rely on these services for a Canadian company; keep the active Canadian run guard. Any US values cited here describe code configuration only, not verified current US tax law.
- **A1 / reviewer blocker:** Select launch scenarios and obtain payroll/tax review before defining Canadian tax/contribution calculations, effective dates, rule-version policy, or mappings from US state concepts.

### 4. Payroll calculation routes and user interface

- **Observed assumption / locations:** The payroll-router calculation entry points are `create_pay_run` ([`runs.py`, L197–237 and L386–446](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L197-L237)) and `gross_up_paycheck` ([`runs.py`, L829–896](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L829-L896)); `/states` is a US state-engine catalog, not a Canadian calculator ([`states.py`, L10–14](../../app/routes/payroll/states.py#L10-L14)). Pay-run creation defaults an omitted work state to employee state or `WA` at L386–389 and passes W-4/state fields and US benefit tax bases into `calculate_withholdings` at L421–446. Gross-up checks jurisdiction against `date.today()` rather than a requested pay date, then uses the employee's US state/W-4 data; its calculator call does not pass benefit or garnishment deductions ([`runs.py`, L846–885](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L846-L885)). A related, standalone overtime classifier is exposed at `/api/time-entries/classify`; its request defaults `state` to `WA` and passes that state to `classify_period` ([`routes/time_entries.py`, L232–242](../../app/routes/time_entries.py#L232-L242)). `services/overtime.py` uses a weekly 40-hour threshold, special daily/double-time rules keyed to US state codes including `CA`, and falls back to the weekly classifier for other codes ([L18–23 and L34–112](../../app/services/overtime.py#L18-L23)). Pay-run creation applies the separate CCPA garnishment engine to disposable earnings; `apply_garnishments` defaults its protected-earnings calculation to a federal-minimum-wage input of `$7.25` ([`runs.py`, L448–454](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L448-L454); [`services/garnishment.py`, L1–15 and L117–160](../../app/services/garnishment.py#L1-L15)). The Payroll page still renders “New Pay Run” and “Calculate Payroll” controls without first selecting a Canadian calculation mode ([`static/js/payroll.js`, L8–17 and L44–85](../../app/static/js/payroll.js#L8-L17)).
- **Current guard:** Active Canadian jurisdiction blocks normal run creation before creating payroll records and blocks gross-up before YTD/tax calculation. Both routes use the shared jurisdiction lock; ambiguous history returns an error and lock failure fails closed. The standalone `/api/time-entries/classify` endpoint has no jurisdiction lookup and applies its US state-code/default behavior independently. Existing tests assert the active-Canada gross-up block and that a future-only jurisdiction permits pre-effective US gross-up ([`test_gross_up_jurisdiction_guard.py`, L45–81](../../tests/test_gross_up_jurisdiction_guard.py#L45-L81)). These are test-source locations, not execution results.
- **Safe interim disposition:** Do not use gross-up, the legacy payroll calculator, the overtime classifier, or the CCPA garnishment engine for Canadian cases; retain server-side run/gross-up blocks. The existing `WA`, `CA`, unknown-state, and federal-default behavior must not be used as a Canadian rule.
- **A1 / reviewer blocker:** Confirm which calculator workflows belong in launch scope, including overtime, supplemental wages, gross-up, and garnishments, and whether pay-date-specific jurisdiction resolution and target-year support are required. No Canadian calculator behavior is selected here.

### 5. Pay-run processing, posting, and payment state

- **Observed assumption / locations:** `process_pay_run` posts a payroll journal entry and marks the run `PROCESSED`; its posting still uses US-oriented federal/state withholding, Social Security, Medicare, FUTA and SUTA payable accounts, with payroll-liability fallbacks, and credits net pay to the bank account ([`runs.py`, L640–648 and L751–820](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L640-L648)). `PROCESSED` is an accounting lifecycle state, not evidence of a payment being made; the current `PayRun` model has no payment state ([`models/payroll.py`, L228–257](../../app/models/payroll.py#L228-L257)).
- **Current guard:** Processing acquires the shared jurisdiction lock before its first database read, resolves jurisdiction using the run's pay date, and holds the lock through posting/commit. Active Canadian jurisdiction or a stored jurisdiction snapshot is rejected before posting (422); ambiguous history returns 409 and lock failure returns 503 ([`runs.py`, L587–616](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L587-L616)). Activation also rejects an effective date on or before an already-processed run's pay date ([`payroll_jurisdiction_service.py`, L198–210](../../app/services/payroll_jurisdiction_service.py#L198-L210)). A pre-existing US-calculated draft whose pay date is later covered by a Canadian range is blocked from processing, but remains a draft; read-side exposure is described in section 9.
- **Safe interim disposition:** Do not treat posting as payment confirmation. Do not process a legacy US-calculated draft when its pay date is covered by Canadian jurisdiction. Do not use these US liability accounts as Canadian account mapping by implication.
- **A1 / reviewer blocker:** Confirm launch payment scenarios and the Canadian posting/remittance-account treatment. Issue #7 calls for manual payment recording in the first release; no payroll-specific payment-recording status or route is present in the inspected model/routes.

- **Observed assumption / locations:** `employee_ytd` aggregates federal, state, state-other, Social Security, and Medicare totals from calendar-year stubs; `_ytd_stubs` excludes void runs but otherwise does not require processed status ([`routes/payroll/ytd.py`, L18–55](../../app/routes/payroll/ytd.py#L18-L55)). - **US forms/reports:** Payroll routes generate W-2/W-3, 940, and 941 JSON/PDF outputs ([`routes/payroll/tax_forms.py`, L27–164 and L211–265](../../app/routes/payroll/tax_forms.py#L27-L164)); the forms include employee SSN-last-four and employer EIN output fields ([`routes/payroll/tax_forms.py`, L57–64](../../app/routes/payroll/tax_forms.py#L57-L64); [`services/payroll_documents.py`, L18–30](../../app/services/payroll_documents.py#L18-L30)). `/api/tax-forms` exposes W-2/W-3, 940/941, SUI, quarterly liability, and separate 1099-NEC/1096 vendor-reporting routes ([`routes/tax_forms.py`, L43–143](../../app/routes/tax_forms.py#L43-L143)). The reviewed W-2/940/941 services aggregate US-labeled stub fields by date/status without a jurisdiction-snapshot predicate; for example, W-2 box totals use federal/state and Social Security/Medicare fields ([`services/tax_forms/w2_w3.py`, L20–74](../../app/services/tax_forms/w2_w3.py#L20-L74)), and 940/941 use FUTA/FICA fields ([`services/tax_forms/form_940.py`, L20–41](../../app/services/tax_forms/form_940.py#L20-L41); [`form_941.py`, L23–27 and L43–61](../../app/services/tax_forms/form_941.py#L23-L27)). This disposition is not fully enforced today: YTD and pay-stub routes lack the jurisdiction guard described above. The existing Canadian design's reporting-artifact section is a candidate design reference only, not launch confirmation; see [`canadian-payroll.md`, “Reporting artifacts and operating boundary”](canadian-payroll.md#reporting-artifacts-and-operating-boundary).
- **A1 / reviewer blocker:** Confirm target tax year and reporting/payment scenarios. Decide Canadian YTD inclusion rules, pay-statement fields, required artifacts, and review/sign-off before a Canadian report path is enabled. Do not treat this inventory as a T4/CRA/ROE specification.
