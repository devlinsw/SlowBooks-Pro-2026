# A2: Inventory of U.S. Payroll Assumptions

> **A1 SCOPE OWNER-APPROVED; A2 DESIGN / IMPLEMENTATION IN PROGRESS; NOT RELEASE-READY.** This is an inventory of U.S. payroll assumptions and current interim guards—not approval to use any U.S. component for Canadian payroll, and not a Canadian-readiness assessment.

**Reviewed source candidate:** `a5ad297b76216b310f174a6dd7211fb9ff6bbde2` (`canada/a2-us-inventory-reconciled`). This identifies the source inspected, not independent quality approval.

## Purpose and decision boundary

A1 scope decisions are recorded in [`canadian-payroll.md`, “Approved boundary”](canadian-payroll.md#approved-boundary); that document's regulatory source matrix is intentionally not repeated here. The confirmed launch boundary is provincially regulated employment in AB, BC, MB, NB, NL, NS, ON, PE, and SK only; Québec, the territories, and federally regulated work are excluded, and federal cases must be blocked. Each company runs payroll in one country at a time; province of employment is assigned per employee and pay period. Supported employee categories are part-time hourly, full-time hourly, and salaried employees. The target year is 2026; approved frequencies are daily, weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, and monthly. Currency is CAD; printable cheques and pay statements are in scope. Statutory filing remains manual: no direct CRA or Service Canada submission, ROE Web filing, bank file, or direct-deposit integration is in scope. See the approved boundary for the full wording and exclusions.

This is a code inventory, not legal, tax, or payroll advice. **No Canadian formulas, rates, or legal conclusions are supplied.** The approved scope is not reviewer sign-off. Reviewer and release gates remain as recorded in [`canadian-payroll.md`, “Testing, review, and release gates”](canadian-payroll.md#testing-review-and-release-gates).

## Component inventory

### 1. Company jurisdiction, U.S. state codes, and fallback

- **Observed assumption / locations:** Company payroll jurisdiction is effective-dated country-mode history; it does not select a province. Province of employment is a separate per-employee timeline. See [`PayrollJurisdiction`](../../app/models/payroll_jurisdiction.py#L1-L40), [`EmployeePayrollJurisdiction`](../../app/models/employee_payroll_jurisdiction.py#L20-L60), and the company/employee history APIs in [`payroll_jurisdiction.py`](../../app/routes/payroll_jurisdiction.py#L126-L154), [L157–203](../../app/routes/payroll_jurisdiction.py#L157-L203). U.S. state `CA` is California in the separate state-tax registry; unknown or missing state codes still select a zero-rate generic engine ([`state_tax/__init__.py`, registry and `get_engine`](../../app/services/state_tax/__init__.py#L36-L61); [`generic.py`](../../app/services/state_tax/generic.py#L18-L46)). Do not equate state `CA` with Canadian country mode `CA`.
- **Current guard:** Pay-run creation and processing resolve company country mode against the complete inclusive period, not only the pay date. A period crossing the U.S.–Canadian country-mode effective date is rejected; a Canadian period is blocked before calculation or posting. With no effective jurisdiction for a wholly earlier period, the legacy U.S. path remains available. See [`resolve_company_payroll_jurisdiction_for_period`](../../app/services/payroll_jurisdiction_service.py#L178-L234), [`create_pay_run`](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L292-L327), and [`process_pay_run`](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L699-L732).
- **Safe interim disposition:** Keep Canadian payroll calculation blocked. Missing or future-only jurisdiction history is legacy U.S. behavior, not evidence that Canadian payroll is supported. Do not use the generic state fallback for an unsupported Canadian province or territory.
- **Remaining design / review / implementation gate:** The company country-mode guard is not a province-level calculator. Connect approved per-employee province resolution and reviewed rule sets before any Canadian calculation is enabled. Same-period cross-province work remains blocked pending design and review; the existing employee resolver rejects a period intersecting multiple provinces, but the current payroll calculator does not call it ([`resolve_employee_payroll_jurisdiction`](../../app/services/payroll_jurisdiction_service.py#L522-L566); current run stub construction at [`runs.py`](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L637-L664)).

### 5. Pay-run processing, posting, and payment state

- **Observed assumption / locations:** `process_pay_run` posts an accounting journal entry and marks the run `PROCESSED`; that status is not evidence of payment. Posting uses U.S.-oriented federal/state/FICA/FUTA/SUTA liabilities and account fallbacks ([`process_pay_run`](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L699-L740), [L750–820](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L750-L820), [L935–943](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L935-L943)). `PayRun` has no payroll payment status/date field ([`models/payroll.py`](../../app/models/payroll.py#L228-L257)).
- **Current guard:** Processing locks jurisdiction history and resolves the run's full inclusive period; Canadian mode, a stored company-jurisdiction snapshot, a cross-country boundary, ambiguous history, or lock failure blocks posting ([`runs.py`](../../app/routes/payroll/runs.py#L704-L732)). Company-mode activation also refuses a processed run if its pay date **or period end** is on/after the proposed effective date ([`add_company_payroll_jurisdiction`](../../app/services/payroll_jurisdiction_service.py#L300-L323)); employee-province assignment checks for processed periods whose period end is on/after its proposed effective date ([`add_employee_payroll_jurisdiction`](../../app/services/payroll_jurisdiction_service.py#L423-L471)).
- **Safe interim disposition:** Do not treat posting as payment confirmation or use U.S. liability accounts as Canadian mappings. The A1 payment boundary is settled: CAD cheques/statements are in scope; bank-file/direct-deposit integration and statutory submissions are not. This does not supply Canadian remittance or accounting mappings.
- **Remaining design / review / implementation gate:** Define and implement manual payment recording, Canadian expense/liability/reconciliation treatment, and remittance/account mapping with accounting and payroll review. Do not infer these from `PROCESSED` or the U.S. posting accounts.
